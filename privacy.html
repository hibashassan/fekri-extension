Fekri extension: your data

The Fekri browser extension copies the chats you choose into your own Fekri. Today it works on WhatsApp Web. Updated 11 November 2026.

Who runs it

The Collective Mind FZE, Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, UAE. Questions and deletion requests: h.hassan@thecollectivemind.me.

What it reads

  • The messages in the chats you choose on WhatsApp Web: who wrote them, when, and the text. Photos, voice notes and documents come through as a label, not the file.
  • It only reads what WhatsApp Web already shows in your browser. It never types, never sends, and never opens a chat with unread messages, so no one gets a read receipt from it.
  • It reads nothing on any other website.

Where it sends it

  • Only to the Fekri address you enter, using the key you made in your Fekri. Nothing goes to us, to advertisers or to anyone else.
  • The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). The key can only add messages to your Fekri; it cannot read your messages back.

What it keeps in your browser

  • Your Fekri address, your key, and a count of copied messages, in this Chrome profile only.
  • Disconnect in the extension removes them. Removing the extension removes everything it kept.

What it never does

  • Never sells your data or uses it for advertising.
  • Never uses your data to train AI models.
  • Never records your screen, keystrokes, microphone or location.
  • Never loads code from outside the extension.

Inside your Fekri

Once the messages reach your Fekri, the Fekri privacy terms apply: your copy and its database are yours alone, message contents are encrypted at rest, and disconnecting the account deletes what was copied from it. To delete your whole Fekri, email us and we remove it within 7 days.

Chrome Web Store

The use of information received by this extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
